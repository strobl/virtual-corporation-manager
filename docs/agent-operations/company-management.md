# Your first company

Begin with the company you want to manage. Use the installation instructions for the exact VCM release or review candidate you received, then open the local address printed by the application.

## Create the company

1. Open **Corporations** and choose **Create your company** in an empty workspace, or **Create company** when companies already exist. The sidebar's **Create corporation** control opens the same form.
2. Enter **Company name**. **Purpose** is optional; you can add it later. No template, short code, department, teammate or runtime connection is required.
3. Choose **Continue**, inspect the save review, then choose **Create company**. Use **Back** to correct the draft before saving. The saved company's member view opens in its company context.

An empty company is a valid starting point. Add only the people and agents you intend to manage. Example names and hours shown in review screenshots are fictional, not a team or ledger that should be created for you.

## Add the team

Choose **Add member** or **Add your first member**. Enter a name and role, then choose **Human** or **AI agent** under **Member type**. Add working context and responsibilities when useful; organization and reporting details can be added as the company grows. Review the change and choose **Add member** to save.

Select the saved member in **Members**. The inspector keeps the company visible and shows the member's type, role, responsibilities, manager and company assignments. Use **Edit member** to make a reviewed change. See [members and responsibilities](members-and-responsibilities.md) for a short role-writing pattern and shared-member guidance.

Use **Reporting lines** to understand who reports to whom. Follow a manager shown in the same company's team to inspect that person or agent. A manager outside the current team is identified as such; changing the reporting relationship does not silently change company membership.

## Keep hours with the right member

Open **Time Tracker** from the company's hours summary to see the company ledger across its members. **Log time** inside a member's inspector keeps that member selected; check company, member, date and the entry's basis before saving.

The existing Time Tracker behavior is unchanged: explicit or estimated delivery hours, reference catalog, corrections, history, analytics and exports. Recorded delivery hours remain separate from runtime duration and result acceptance. Setup, membership edits and reporting lines create no hours automatically. Returning from Time Tracker should keep the relevant company context; returning to a member on a narrow screen should leave that inspector visible and focused.

## Return and manage

Open the company again from **Your companies**. Search its members by name, role or responsibility and use the Agents/Humans filter when useful. Open **Edit company** for identity or purpose changes. Add departments and company relationships only when your organization needs them.

For an AI agent, **Give a task** is secondary to its member details and requires a ready runtime and eligible company context. A human has **Record work**, not an agent execution control. **Tools → Agent runs & records** and specialized workflow examples remain available when needed. Neither is a prerequisite for managing the company.

If saving fails or the displayed company changes unexpectedly, retain the error and follow [support](../support.md). A draft or review screen is not a saved company; confirm the saved member view before calling setup complete.

The full [Time Tracker guide](../time-tracker.md) covers existing entry rules, estimates, corrections, exports and recovery. For an optional specialized workflow, use [Product Studio](../product-studio.md).
