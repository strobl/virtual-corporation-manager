# The corporation is the product

VCM is where an owner creates and maintains a virtual corporation: its identity, people and agents, responsibilities, reporting structure, company relationships and recorded delivery hours. The organization persists across work sessions. Opening VCM should make that organization understandable and editable immediately.

The founder's reference to Stripe Atlas and Carta is a product-model reference. Atlas organizes a service around creating a company; Carta organizes equity administration around companies, stakeholders and securities. The useful lesson is to build around the thing the customer manages. It does not make VCM a legal incorporation service or an equity register. Sources: [Stripe Atlas documentation](https://docs.stripe.com/atlas), [Carta company navigation](https://releasenotes.carta.com/label/23491).

## The everyday job

“I want to open my virtual corporation, understand who belongs to it and what each member is responsible for, change the organization when needed, and keep its contributions attached to the right company.”

The core loop is create a corporation → add agents and people → define responsibilities and relationships → return to manage the same corporation. Agent execution is a capability of a member. It is not the organizing object of the product.

## Interaction decisions

- **One company context.** Selecting a company opens its membership and structure. Selecting a member keeps the company visible and opens its details beside it. Basic management does not require switching between an overview and a second organization application.
- **Create first, elaborate when needed.** A company needs a name; purpose is optional. Create an empty company, then add its real team. Internal codes and colors receive valid defaults. Departments and hierarchy are available when the organization needs them.
- **People and agents are both members.** Creating a member exposes that choice. Name and role are immediate; working context, responsibilities and reporting details are progressively disclosed. A human never receives an executable-agent control.
- **Keep relationships precise.** Membership answers which company a member belongs to; reporting answers who manages whom; ownership connects companies. Sharing a member does not create a new identity. Company ownership percentages are not human shareholdings or a legal cap table.
- **Time Logging stays.** Preserve the existing Time Tracker, entry model, estimates, catalog, corrections, history and exports. A compact company summary leads to the complete existing tool. Runtime duration does not become recorded delivery hours.
- **Tools serve the company.** Agent runs, workflow examples and runtime connections are secondary utilities. A fixed stock-alert example does not stand in for the customer's corporation. The company does not need a task board to be useful.

## What to judge in a product review

A returning owner should recognize their company and find a member without learning the implementation. Creating a human, changing a responsibility, following a reporting line, inspecting company relationships and opening the company's Time Tracker should be obvious. Selection must preserve company context, including for shared members. Empty states, long names, narrow screens and failed saves should receive the same care as a populated desktop view.

The technical execution boundary remains explicit: general agent runs produce read-only text through a configured runtime; reporting lines do not automatically delegate work. Existing specialized workflows keep their current limits. This product refinement neither invents autonomy nor substitutes a new project-management system for corporation management.
