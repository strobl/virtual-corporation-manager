# VCM brand brief

VCM means Virtual Corporation Manager. GitFlash is a separate FDE boutique. Product: a local open-source workspace for your virtual corporation, with humans and AI agents, responsibilities, reporting lines and company relationships.

Brand line: **Big ideas deserve a team.** Campaign: **Solo. Never small.** Attitude: **Build what comes next.** Values: Play 2 win. Be adaptable. Never stop hacking. Hackers first. Be open.

Audience: developers, solo founders and curious builders organizing their work. Speak in clear, energetic English. Show a real product interaction or a clearly illustrative team structure. Invite people to inspect, adapt, build and share. One idea per post. No generic AI news recaps, fabricated traction, income promises or claims of a company operating autonomously.

Visuals: orange `#FF5A2A`, ink `#17252A`, cream `#F6F3E8`, cobalt `#2D4DF0`, mint `#A8DED0`, butter `#F7D85B`. Rubik Bold for headlines. Native VCM wordmark paths. Original rounded V-shaped companion, orange hero with mint/butter teammates. It is called Vic in the source brand system; preserve its original identity and geometry. Mascot artwork is editorial, not a product screenshot or a human colleague.

Use the canonical domain virtualcorporationmanager.com when it serves the accepted product landing page. Until then use the verified repository and versioned release. The current public archive is vcm-0.1.0-alpha.8.tgz, a technical alpha. Recheck the version for every later release post.

Product boundaries: local SQLite core, one operator, no required account for core organization and Time Tracker. Optional provider execution has separate setup. Humans remain distinguishable from AI agents. An org chart does not automatically dispatch work. Preserve MIT-core wording and the font's OFL notice.
