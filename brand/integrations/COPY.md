## Bring your VCM team into Buzz.

Buzz.xyz · Draft

[Image](exports/05-meet-the-channel.png)

Big ideas deserve a team. Give yours a room.

VCM is an open-source workspace for organizing a corporation with humans and AI agents. Its Buzz export carries active AI roles and instructions into a native .team.json.

Select your corporation → Tools → Connections → Export Buzz team. In Buzz Desktop, preview the import before confirming. Check the runtime before starting agents.

The export transfers configuration. The optional task connection is experimental; a live useful-result proof is still open.

Start here: https://github.com/strobl/virtual-corporation-manager/blob/main/docs/integrations/buzz.md

**Alt text:** Big ideas. Meet the channel. Organize your team in VCM and explore optional Buzz.xyz and Slack connections. Team import; experimental task connections. The original orange Vic appears on cream.

## One role. One focused brief.

Slack · Draft

[Image](exports/03-slack-mention.png)

A mention. A mission.

VCM organizes the team. Its experimental Slack adapter gives one allowed owner a way to address a named AI role in one channel. Local Codex runs the task; the result returns to its thread and stays in VCM for review.

The repo now includes a Slack app manifest, a VCM app icon and a setup guide. Configure your own app and runtime, then explicitly connect in Tools → Connections.

Live execution acceptance is still open. The illustrated example is a walkthrough, not a real delivery receipt.
